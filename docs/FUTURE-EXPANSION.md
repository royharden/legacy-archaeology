# Future expansion: precision clarification

Decision recorded: 2026-10-25, PB-M1-R03.

Roy explicitly chose at 6:06 PM ET to retain the current precision-contract
limitation for this run. His 6:08 PM ET addition requested this durable roadmap.
This is a deliberate scope decision. This note does not authorize implementation
or amend the selected plan, question protocol, evaluator, or acceptance criteria.
A separate expansion requires a new explicit grant from Roy.

## Current boundary

Answers select an offered complete-policy candidate ID or unknown. Selected limits are at most three policy alternatives per field, twelve
submitted questions per layout and three attempted product inference requests. Missing intended
precision must remain unknown, incomplete and unapproved. Equal decoded sample
values do not establish the intended target-domain precision. An unoffered ID
cannot be invented or replaced with the nearest offered policy.

The accepted negative regression preserves that boundary. The known-public
12-row/60-field journey is a separate scoped result; it does not close broader
six-variant inference quality. Retain the existing full-denominator reports,
including zero exact rows out of 5,820 per arm, without relabeling them.

## Possible later direction

The smaller reviewed proposal would introduce a separately typed, finite
intended-precision question before the final complete-policy questions. It
would offer at most three distinct code-generated precision choices plus
unknown, deduplicating equal choices. Unknown or absent precision would end
that bounded phase; menus would not rotate to enumerate more values.

One explicitly confirmed precision could then generate at most three complete
policies per field. Five final semantic rulings and complete mapping approval
would still be required. A precision fact alone would authorize neither typed
row output nor loading. Other missing policy axes could still prevent completion;
this proposal carries no quality guarantee.

Ternary refinement over supported precision values 1 through 1,000 is a broader,
separate proposal. Its worst case requires seven precision answers plus five
final semantic answers, consuming all twelve slots before any extra prerequisite
or changed-dependency question. Arithmetic feasibility is not protocol approval.
It is not included in the smaller proposal or authorized by this roadmap.

## Prerequisites for any new grant and implementation

- Roy must explicitly approve the follow-on scope and a versioned plan/protocol
  amendment, including the relationship between precision-fact choices and the
  existing three complete-policy alternatives. The broader range design requires
  its own explicit decision.
- Specify a closed typed question/answer interface, permitted information from
  an actual domain owner, and separately versioned evaluator permission for a
  simulated owner. Samples alone cannot supply the missing domain constraint.
  Preserve offered-ID-only responses, legacy wire/hash readers and isolation of
  additional R1 information from all other arms.
- Define a server-owned durable assessment identity and submission ledger shared
  across phases, generations, retries and restarts. Count unanswered, unknown,
  timed-out and failed-delivery submissions within twelve. Exact retries return
  historical receipts; concurrent writers cannot spend the last slot twice.
  Preserve the separate three-attempt inference ledger and truthful exhaustion.
- Bind precision facts to source, framing, brief, permitted samples, semantic/span
  context and protocol/policy versions. Resolve prerequisites under the same
  budget or abstain. Revisions invalidate dependent generations, rulings and
  approvals; historical receipts cannot authorize changed work.
- Require reviewer-owned durability, CAS, crash/replay, stale-event, budget,
  invalid-input and arm-isolation regressions, plus independent target/catalog
  precision checks even when short sample values match. Preserve the current
  absent-policy regression unchanged. Reconcile producer and consumer interfaces
  before independently reviewed integration and combined acceptance.
- Freeze a new evaluation version before claiming improvement. Preserve all old
  results and disclosed information costs. No hidden/live benchmark or full
  product-success claim follows from design review or arithmetic alone.
